Put song audio here so the record on /notesbyaryan plays it on the page (no Instagram).

1. Export the song from your own recording (the reel's original video works too): .m4a or .mp3 is best, .mp4 also plays.
   Keep it small: a 64–96 kbps m4a of a 1–2 min cover is about 1 MB.
2. Name it simply, e.g. `aankhein-teri.m4a`, and save it in this folder.
3. In content/music.json, add `"audio": "/audio/aankhein-teri.m4a"` to that song.
   The record plays `musicCopy.featuredCode` (content/music.ts); change that code to feature another song.

Without a file, clicking the record opens the song's sheet with the Instagram reel instead.
